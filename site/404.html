Public engineering MVP

CrisisWeave

A modular prototype for turning fragmented crisis information into a provenance-preserving operational picture, while keeping volunteer recovery work explicit and separate from hazard inference.

Coordinator view

Incident evidence, provenance, verification and alert triage for information-management users.

Volunteer work

Concrete requested or assessed worksites with crew needs, skills, hazards and assignment state.

Access boundary

Organisation-scoped roles, hashed bearer tokens, private-data separation and audit through crisisweave-platform.

Safety: CrisisWeave is not an emergency authority and must not be the sole source for evacuation, medical, fire, police or rescue decisions. The public demo uses synthetic data.
